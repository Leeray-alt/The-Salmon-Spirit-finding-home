About the Memoir

In The Salmon's Spirit: Finding Home, Dr. Similo Ngwenya tells a story of struggle and triumph, of a young girl growing up in the fractured landscape of a broken African home. Caught in a web of suspicion, fear, and deeply held cultural beliefs that shaped her parents' choices, she learned early on how to survive in a world that often felt senseless and unforgiving.


As she enters adulthood, armed with education, faith and an unyielding determination to break free from the cycles of her past, she finds herself confronting the very forces she once sought to escape. Is there a formula for survival? Or is life a journey of constant adaptation and renewal?

"But more importantly, I learned that home is not always something we stumble upon; sometimes it is something we create. I found home within myself. A state of being where I felt safe, grounded and reassured that I mattered. This sense of belonging was not just about physical safety, but about reaching a place of enough..."

— Epilogue: Lessons in resilience (p. 172)

Meet the Author

Dr. Similo Ngwenya is a scholar currently based in the United Kingdom. A researcher in the interdisciplinary field of science and technology studies, she holds a PhD from Stellenbosch University in South Africa.


She has published several articles on a range of meta-scientific topics, including science policy, inequalities in science, research collaboration, evaluation, and impact.
